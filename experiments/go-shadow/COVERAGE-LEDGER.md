# Go IAST taint tracking — coverage ledger

Derived from `stock-vs-orchestrion.html` (Go IAST research report, July 2026) and then
**resolved empirically**: every case the report marked *Not proven* was handed to a dedicated
agent that wrote a real, executing test and recorded the measured outcome.

Cells marked ✎ were measured in this exercise. Unmarked cells are the report's original claims.

| Prototype | Suite | Subtests before | Subtests after |
| --- | --- | ---: | ---: |
| Orchestrion (stock Go) | `runtime/taint/instrument/` | 35 assertions in 1 test | 174 subtests across 3 tests (86 instrumented + 86 under `-race` + 2 disabled-mode) |
| Patched Go | `experiments/go-shadow/suite/` | 31 subtests | 97 subtests |

## Result

| Prototype | Win | Loss | Partial | Not proven |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Orchestrion — before | 54 | 35 | 12 | 54 |
| **Orchestrion — after** | **103** | **35** | **12** | **5** |
| Patched Go — before | 44 | 30 | 9 | 72 |
| **Patched Go — after** | **49** | **89** | **9** | **8** |

113 cells measured across 155 cases.
